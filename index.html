MAUI MARATHON · UPCOMING EVENT

Run the race. Know how your body moves.

POWERED BY RUNEASI

Lab-grade running gait & jumping analysis. Made easy.

RunEasi uses a small wearable sensor to capture real-time data on how you move while running — impact loading, symmetry, cadence, ground contact time, and more. In just a few minutes, you get a clear picture of where your body is compensating, and what that means for injury risk.

Whether you are training for your first race or your fifteenth, this assessment gives you actionable insights so you can run smarter, recover faster, and stay on the road longer.

✓Left/right symmetry analysis
